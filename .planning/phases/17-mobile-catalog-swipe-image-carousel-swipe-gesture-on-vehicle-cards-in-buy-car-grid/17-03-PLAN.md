<task type="checkpoint:human-verify" gate="blocking">
  <what-built>
Image carousel swipe gesture in VehicleCard.tsx. Cards with 2+ images now show:
- A horizontal swipe gesture that advances/retreats the image by 1 per swipe
- Small indicator dots (4px, white filled/outline) at the bottom centre of the image
- Rubber-band resistance past the first and last image
- One light haptic per image change
- Tap still navigates to listing detail
- Vertical scroll in FlatList is unimpeded
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
1. Build and install the app on a physical device (run `npx expo start` or your standard dev flow)
2. Open the "Buy a Car" / Search screen — this renders VehicleCard in a FlatList
3. Find a listing card with 2 or more images (dots should be visible at the bottom of the image)
4. **Swipe test:** Swipe left on the image — it should advance to image 2 with a spring snap. Swipe right — it should return to image 1.
5. **Fast flick:** Flick quickly left — image advances even without dragging 30% of the width.
6. **Threshold snap-back:** Slowly drag left less than 30% of the card width, then release — image springs back to original.
7. **Rubber-band:** Swipe left repeatedly until you reach the last image, then swipe left again — you should feel slight drag resistance and see the image spring back (no hard stop).
8. **Haptics:** Each image advance should produce one subtle "tick" haptic. No haptic should fire during drag, only on snap completion.
9. **Dots:** Dots at the bottom should highlight the active image. Dots count should match the number of images (max 5).
10. **Tap test:** Tap (do not swipe) the card — it should navigate to the listing detail screen.
11. **Vertical scroll test:** In the SearchScreen FlatList, scroll vertically — no stutter or scroll capture by the card gesture.
12. **Single-image card:** Find a listing with only 1 image — it should show no dots and swipe gesture should have no effect (parent FlatList scroll works normally).
  </how-to-verify>
  <resume-signal>Type "approved" if all 12 checks pass, or describe any issues (e.g. "tap not working", "dots missing", "rubber-band feels like hard stop")</resume-signal>
</task>
