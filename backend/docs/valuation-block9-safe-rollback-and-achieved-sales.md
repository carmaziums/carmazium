### Flag requirements

```dotenv
VALUATION_CALIBRATION_MODE=off
VALUATION_CALIBRATION_DATA_REVIEW_APPROVED=false
VALUATION_CALIBRATION_ROLLOUT_APPROVED=false
VALUATION_CALIBRATION_AUDITED_AUCTION_IDS=
```

- `off` (default): do not query historical calibration outcomes; do not change customer prices. OFF wins even for an existing previously calibrated immutable base.
- `shadow`: only when data-review permission is explicitly approved. Run a read-only, bounded historical evaluation against **seller-confirmed, admin-approved handovers**; do not change money, customer confidence or automatically apply reserves. Raw outcomes and audit IDs are not logged or exposed.
- `on`: requires **all** of data review approved, live rollout approved, a list of at least 30 **individually inspected/audited** auction listing UUIDs, a MODEL_VERIFIED request, a newly calculated live/blended base, and a passing independent holdout evaluation for that narrow vehicle cohort. A mere global approval flag does not certify individual financial events: the audit manifest is mandatory. If any requirement fails or optional DB reads time out, prices remain unchanged. The individually audited IDs are server-side restricted configuration, never copied into docs, client code, logs or analytics.

## Achieved sale provenance and matching

The only currently eligible channel is **auction**. A record must be: CAR/AUCTION listing with matching exact make/model, same variant, manufacture year within one year, mileage within 20,000 miles, completed SOLD listing and ENDED auction, paid buyer fee, valid winner, no buyer refusal, approved handover with a timestamp, and seller-confirmed funds (or explicit legacy exemption). The seller's confirmation is an **attestation**, NOT independent proof of a bank transfer: operators must verify each record in the ON manifest against supporting evidence. An accepted bid, generic historical Sale row, vehicle advert or provider benchmark is never a confirmed achieved auction result.

Each eligible distinct vehicle must have an actual **earlier** immutable valuation snapshot for the matching registration/year/model/mileage (including separately keyed variant where relevant). The snapshot must carry a **prospectively recorded** verified-at-creation marker and a LIVE_UK_MARKET or BLENDED_MARKET source. A snapshot written after the auction starts is ineligible, preventing an outcome from leaking into its own prediction. Historical snapshots from before this marker was introduced cannot silently be declared verified; wait for fresh eligible evidence or a separately approved import with independently audited provenance. The current query vehicle is excluded from its own calibration cohort. A car repeated across listing records counts **once**. The in-memory evaluator uses only opaque SHA-256 identity and predicted/achieved numeric pairs. Only aggregate results leave the loader; raw matching rows/VRMs and all seller data are discarded after evaluation. No new paid provider, scraping arrangement or database branch is created.

**Chronological holdout:** at least 30 distinct eligible vehicles with predictions preceding outcomes, split into at least 20 earlier training sales and 10 later held-out sales. Median training outcome/prediction ratio is constrained to a **maximum ±10% auction-only correction**. The independent later holdout must achieve at least 1.5 **percentage-point** improvement in mean absolute percentage error (MAPE), corrected MAPE <=15%, and at least 80% of held-out predictions within ±20% of achieved price. These deliberately conservative predeclared thresholds are **not a proof of statistical calibration**; validate more broadly across geography, trims, market cycles and UK seasons before customer rollout. Low sample count, incoherent prices, changed-market drift or out-of-range outcomes fail closed.

The calibration affects **only** `auction.marketValue`, `auction.openingBid`, `auction.reserveLow`, `auction.reserveHigh` and `auction.suggestedReserve`. It never overwrites broad `low/mid/high`, retail/private channel guides, the customer's manually entered amount, operational 70% opening-bid listing rule, seller handover, buyer payment or existing confidence rubrics. Block 8 HIGH evidence strength remains withheld; passing this small holdout is not sufficient to promise prediction accuracy.
