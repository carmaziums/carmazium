Carmazium Website Improvement
Requirements and Strategic
Recommendations
1. Auction Policy
● Auctions have been reviewed and the decision has been made not to proceed with
them at this stage.
2. Seller Transparency and Reliability
● Display all vehicles listed by individual sellers to enhance customer trust and seller
reliability.
● Provide a seller reliability score or performance history where applicable.
3. Sales and Trading Options
● Temporarily integrate an AutoTrader-style system that supports:
○ Direct vehicle sales (Buy & Sell)
○ Auction functionality (if reintroduced in the future)
● Allow vehicles to be listed for both auction and direct trade simultaneously.
● No upfront payment should be required from buyers or sellers at the listing stage.
4. Vehicle Listing Requirements
● Mandatory VIN number entry at the first step to enable easy and accurate data input.
● Integration of AI-generated vehicle descriptions based on provided information.
● Minimum requirement of 30 high-quality images per listing:
○ Indoor and outdoor photographs
○ Movable and rearrangeable image placement
● Inclusion of the seller’s current geolocation.
● Consider environmental compliance requirements relevant to UK regulations when
introducing initial listing steps.
5. Website Features and User Experience
● Enable dark mode and light mode options.
● Allow users to browse and search vehicles without logging in.
● Require user login to access detailed vehicle information.
● Focus on a lightweight, fast, and user-friendly website structure.
● Prioritize bug fixes and seamless navigation.
● Ensure robust database setup and storage management.
6. Advanced Search and Filter Functionality
Sell Car Section – Filtering Options:
● Location:
○ Nearest first
○ Farthest first
● Price:
○ Lowest to highest
○ Highest to lowest
○ Slider-based price range selection
● Mileage:
○ Lowest to highest
○ Highest to lowest
● Vehicle condition (including CAT classifications)
● “Best for You” AI-powered recommendations based on:
○ Recent searches
○ User behavior
○ Market trends
7. Marketing Strategy
Digital Strategy:
● Develop and implement marketing strategies through Bitroix, including a clear fee
and pricing structure.
● Conduct market research on AutoTrader regions where Carmazium can enter and
compete effectively.
● Initial geographic focus: London.
● Implement professional SEO services to improve organic search rankings and
visibility.
8. Data Collection and Lead Generation
● Allow open browsing to increase traffic.
● Require login for detailed access to collect user data and generate leads.
● Strategically gather customer data for future marketing campaigns and potential lead
monetization.
9. Priority Focus Areas
● Eliminate technical bugs.
● Maintain a clean, modern, and intuitive interface.
● Ensure fast loading speed and mobile responsiveness.
● Deliver a light, efficient, and scalable platform
