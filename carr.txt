Step 1 — Get the Base Market Price
Use the average of similar cars in the UK market.
Filter criteria
Make
Model
Year ±1
Fuel type
Transmission
Body type
SQL Example
SELECT AVG(price) as base_price
FROM cars
WHERE make = :make
AND model = :model
AND year BETWEEN :year-1 AND :year+1
AND fuel_type = :fuel
AND transmission = :transmission;
Example:
Mercedes C-Class 2016
BasePrice = £15,000

2️⃣ Final UK Car Price Formula
EstimatedPrice =
BasePrice

+ FuelAdjustment
+ OwnershipAdjustment
+ LocationDemandAdjustment

3️⃣ Mileage Adjustment (UK standard)
Average UK mileage ≈ 10,000 miles/year
ExpectedMileage = Age × 10000
MileageDiff = ActualMileage − ExpectedMileage

MileageAdjustment = MileageDiff × (-0.06)
Example:
Age = 8
ExpectedMileage = 80000
ActualMileage = 60000

MileageDiff = -20000

MileageAdjustment = +£1200

4️⃣ Age Depreciation
Used cars lose about 6% per year in the UK market.
AgeAdjustment = BasePrice × (-0.06 × Age)
Example:
BasePrice = 15000
Age = 8

AgeAdjustment = -£7200

5️⃣ Condition Adjustment
Excellent = +10%
Good = +5%
Average = 0
Fair = -10%
Poor = -20%
Formula
ConditionAdjustment = BasePrice × ConditionRate

6️⃣ Service History Adjustment
Full service history matters a lot in the UK.
Full Service History = +5%
Partial History = +2%
No History = -5%
ServiceAdjustment = BasePrice × ServiceRate

7️⃣ Transmission Adjustment
Automatic cars are more valuable in the UK.
Automatic = +£1000
Manual = £0

8️⃣ Fuel Type Adjustment (UK market)
Electric = +12%
Hybrid = +6%
Petrol = 0
Diesel = -4%
FuelAdjustment = BasePrice × FuelRate

9️⃣ Ownership Adjustment
Cars with fewer owners sell for more.
1 owner = +5%
2 owners = +2%
3+ owners = -3%
OwnershipAdjustment = BasePrice × OwnerRate

🔟 Location Demand Adjustment (UK)
London / South East = +4%
Major cities = +2%
Rural areas = 0
