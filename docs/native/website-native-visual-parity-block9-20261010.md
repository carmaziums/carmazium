# Issue #477 — Block 9/10: Role gates, accessibility and layout resilience

Date: 2026-10-10. **90% = nine of ten planned source implementation blocks, NOT a measured visual-match score.**

## Source audit findings and fixes

1. **P0: dealer bottom tabs could bypass the navigator-level gate.** Before this block, `MainStackNavigator` wrapped `DealerInventoryScreen` in `withDealerGate(..., 'VIEW_INVENTORY')` and `DealerLeadsScreen` in `withDealerGate(..., 'MANAGE_CRM')`, but `TabNavigator` directly registered the RAW screens. The custom tab bar merely *hid* the buttons. An internal/deep link or preserved navigation state could mount a screen without passing through that existing KYC/staff-permission guard. **FIX:** module-scoped `GatedDealerStockTab`, `GatedDealerCustomersTab`, `GatedDealerBuyBidTab` wrap their original screens with respectively `VIEW_INVENTORY`, `MANAGE_CRM`, `VIEW_TRADE` permissions and verified-dealership checks. Module scope prevents remount churn. Existing client UI filtering remains an extra navigation convenience; backend remains the final authority. Dealer Home retains its established access/onboarding entry.
2. **P1: staff could see dealer-owner settings controls.** `SettingsScreen` considered `accountRole === 'dealer'` sufficient to show Business profile, business KYC and owner-oriented account-action links even for `user.isDealerStaff`. **FIX:** `canManageBusiness = isDealerAccount && !isDealerStaff` gates business section and its deep links, KYC business-management UI and business verification action. Staff retain their personal details, notifications and personal verification view, but do not see management of dealership legal identity. `Account type` identifies staff as `Dealer team member` and displays owner-only instructions rather than role-switch control. Staff membership never elevates own account into ownership. **Do not change server KYC or company permissions.** Personal payouts remain governed by their existing identity-specific backend flow (do not assume an employee's personal bank data is company-wide).
3. **P1: large-text bottom tabs would silently shrink to unreadable 60% fonts.** Original tab label `numberOfLines={1}`, `adjustsFontSizeToFit` and `minimumFontScale={0.6}` could hide long labels at 200% system text. **FIX:** wrap to two lines and allow 2× native font scaling, increase tab-item heights at font scale 1.3/1.6, preserve 44dp or larger touch targets, and share exactly the same computed bottom-tab bar height with the dealer More panel so it stays above the actual bar. Do not accidentally reduce the other tabs' route accessibility semantics. Pure `nativeLayoutParity.ts` supplies breakpoints and single-column settings decisions.
4. **P1: fixed-height dealership navigation clipped on increased font scale.** CRM's horizontal stage strip was fixed to max 54dp and its list/board icon targets were 32×32dp; dealer Buy & Bid's five-destination strip max 58dp. **FIX:** both nav strips now expand with system font scale, CRM list/grid toggles have 44×44dp targets; selected-state semantics and original navigation callbacks remain unchanged. Horizontally scrollable controls preserve access to all six CRM stages and all five Buy & Bid destinations rather than hiding them under an overflow menu.
5. **P2: header and keyboard double-inset.** Messages received `WebsiteTopBar` in Block 8 but still padded its own list by `insets.top + 16`, adding extra blank space on notched devices. **FIX:** list starts at 16dp after the global header; bottom list content includes device bottom inset plus clearance for safe keyboard/scroll, and supports keyboard dismiss on drag/persist taps. Settings scroll also supports keyboard dismiss on drag. Common website header bell and account targets are now 44×44dp; Account Settings Back target minimum 44dp. No chat sending, mark-read or notification mutation logic was changed.
6. **Responsive Settings columns.** Nine account-section tabs and four account shortcuts originally forced two columns on any screen; in 320–359dp width or ≥1.5× font-scale, button labels are now arranged in one column without forcibly reducing system text. On normal 360dp/390dp with standard text, the website-like two-column density is preserved.
