## 1. CarMazium owner's instruction and factual basis

CarMazium has directed that the following two **additional CarMazium-side engineering programmes** will be outside the active SimpleDMS integration programme indefinitely:

1. Commissioning new **independent copies/backups of CarMazium-owned Supabase Storage objects** (public listing photographs, private KYC and handover objects), and separately implementing a dedicated backup runner or paid storage project.
2. Additional **CarMazium-owned isolated database/Storage restore drills and broad production database-role/GraphQL/RLS/privileged-function security-hardening or migration programme**. No database ACL, role or new secret should be changed merely to satisfy that workstream.

**This is deferral, not completion or proof of recoverability or security.** The Supabase Dashboard screenshot provided by CarMazium's owner on 18 October 2026 shows available **PHYSICAL managed database restore points dated 11–18 October** (latest 18 October at 03:08 UTC). **The owner has not executed a test restore.** Supabase expressly states that its managed database backups do **not** include Storage API object bytes. No independent Storage backup is represented or guaranteed.

## 2. Proposed replacement for only the CarMazium-specific independent-recovery acceptance condition

> For its **own production marketplace infrastructure**, CarMazium will provide proportionate, available evidence of its existing managed PostgreSQL recovery-point listings and ordinary operational safeguards. For this free pilot only, **the parties agree that commissioning a new independent backup destination, exporting copies of CarMazium Storage objects, conducting an additional full production-equivalent isolated recovery exercise, or executing a wider database role/GraphQL/RLS hardening programme is not a condition precedent to the Pilot Launch Date**. The parties acknowledge these particular activities have not been completed and do not certify their completeness or adequacy.
>
> This variation does **not** authorise either party to process personal data unlawfully, make inaccurate security representations, undermine source-data accuracy or access controls, skip a legally required risk assessment, or remove rights under UK data-protection law. CarMazium remains responsible for appropriate technical and organisational measures in its own systems. Nothing removes a security measure objectively necessary for safe or lawful processing of the **specific proposed partner feed**.

## 3. SimpleDMS-specific licensed-data protections expressly preserved

> SimpleDMS remains responsible for security, protection, retention, expiry, correction, suppression and deletion of the **CarMazium-origin data in SimpleDMS-controlled systems**, including any authorised subprocessors and recovery copies. SimpleDMS must provide proportionate evidence that its **own** historical-source dataset cannot be reconstituted beyond its expiry or accidentally republished after a recovery operation.
>
> No historical photographs, photograph URLs, seller contact details, private documents, reserves or raw registration are permitted in historical event stores or saved reports. The seven-day independent BuySmart analytical lifecycle and the separate original 36-month historical-event/report ceilings remain subject to both parties' express approval. Source-rights restrictions and required transfer, processor and data-sharing terms remain in force.

## 4. Prerequisites that are NOT proposed for waiver

The following still require documented acceptance **before any licensed production personal/vehicle data sharing**:

- Each party's **lawful basis**, appropriate privacy notices, role allocation and purpose compatibility, including the distinct long-term historical processing and any required DPIA-screening/assessment. Existing listing upload is not deemed blanket GDPR consent. Older unacknowledged listings remain excluded unless a lawful alternative is separately designed and accepted.
- CarMazium's restricted **disabled-by-default partner API**, three independent production-access switches, active/acknowledged listing filters, no private fields, authentication, revocation, image-host/rights allowlists, safe current-live photograph handling, and an actual credential-free 404 denial check before issuing any key.
- SimpleDMS's tested matching, raw-VRM removal, historical event/report expiry, field minimisation, deletion/correction, provider rights and account-specific DPA/transfer evidence. Unapproved DVLA/DVSA, Autotrader and licensed auction-catalogue recipients remain excluded unless separately authorised.
- No new financial commitment; the waived £5,000 fee, free 90-day pilot, no auto-renewal, original 36-month limitations, 30-day convenience-termination provision and the independent confidentiality/liability/incident duties remain unchanged.
- Signatures from both directors and a separately arranged, controlled **live plus historical** acceptance exercise, securely delivered restricted production credential and a jointly written Pilot Launch Date. Signing alone never enables access.

1. **Schedule C6 / C9:** Replace the blanket statement that **both parties must prove new independent database/Storage backups and isolated restoration** with the narrower, transparently incomplete CarMazium provision above, while retaining independently evidenced protection of licensed data **under SimpleDMS's control**.

3. **Contract section 17:** Do **not** waive either party's duty to comply with applicable law and implement appropriate risk-proportionate technical and organisational measures.
4. **Remaining agreement:** Other schedules and agreed commercial, historical-data and termination provisions remain unchanged.
