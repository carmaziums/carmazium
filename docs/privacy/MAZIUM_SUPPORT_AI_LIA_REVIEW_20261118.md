# MaziuM official support: legitimate-interests assessment (owner review)

**Prepared:** 18 November 2026. **Scope:** official authenticated `SUPPORT` conversations only; not buyer/seller private chats, vehicle valuations, search, financing or warranty underwriting.

## Purpose test
CarMazium has a legitimate interest in answering routine marketplace support questions promptly and consistently, reducing queues, and ensuring customers can ask for a person without leaving the chat. No automated AI model may approve a refund, payout, finance, KYC, complaint resolution or account action.

## Necessity test
The proposed limited processing is the latest general support question, with moderation and a short answer generated by a contracted AI provider. A fixed local FAQ answers known questions without sharing content. Staff-only handling is a viable alternative but has significant wait times and cannot automatically answer routine questions out of hours. Only unmatched **non-sensitive** questions are sent to the model. No full account profile, private buyer/seller chats, files, payments or historical conversation is included in this request.

## Balancing test
- **Reasonable expectations:** the customer is in a clearly labelled MaziuM AI support conversation; the composer displays just-in-time notice that routine questions may be sent to OpenAI and links to the privacy policy.
- **Choice and human rights:** an accessible Options control can pause AI immediately and a typed human request routes to staff, without forcing a separate consent form or button. Old opt-outs remain respected. A staff takeover pauses responses.
- **Data minimisation:** only the latest eligible text is processed, never attachments; apparent contact information, payment credentials, health, identity and fraud/complaint/legal issues are routed to human staff. Financial decisions remain human-only.
- **Controls:** dedicated locked bot identity, room-level recheck, per-source idempotency, daily cap, failure fallback, no AI replies in private conversations, model input/output moderation, privacy notice and account rights.
- **Risks:** free-form text can contain unexpected personal/special category information despite filtering; false reassurance/hallucination; third-party and international transfers. Provide ongoing audit, improvements to filtering and a prompt reporting route.

**Provisional conclusion:** a legitimate-interests basis may be appropriate for **routine** official support, if the business documents necessity and balancing and verifies the provider and transfer safeguards. It cannot be assumed for sensitive processing or solely automated decisions with significant consequences. If these conditions fail, suspend external model processing and use human/local FAQ replies until remedied.

## Owner/operations review before or alongside production activation

- [ ] Confirm controller-to-processor terms, OpenAI API data use, retention, security and international transfer mechanism applicable to the actual account.
- [ ] Record the accountable business owner's sign-off of purpose/necessity/balancing and the chosen UK GDPR basis.
- [ ] Verify the provider notice, contact method for privacy objections, support settings and staff escalation route in live buyer/seller/dealer sessions.
- [ ] Sample test personally identifying, special-category and suspicious messages to confirm external model avoidance; document failure monitoring.
- [ ] Review incident handling, retention, audit trail, changes to processing, and whether a full DPIA is warranted.

This is a working compliance assessment for CarMazium decision-makers, not a legal opinion or evidence that outstanding safeguards have been approved.
